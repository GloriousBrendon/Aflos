# Aflos terms of use

**Aflos is for people 18 and over.** By installing or using Aflos you
confirm that you are at least 18 years old. There is no age check; this is
a line you agree to.

## What Aflos is

Aflos is a chat app for close friends. Messages, calls and files go between
your devices and your friends' devices, end-to-end encrypted. There are no
accounts on a server, no public directory, and no way to search for
strangers.

## Nobody at Aflos reads, moderates or stores your chats

There is no central server that holds your messages, so nobody at Aflos can
see them, take them down or hand them over.

- **Block** anyone, at any time. A blocked person can no longer reach you.
- **Spaces are run by their owner.** If someone in a Space is abusive, block
  them and report them to the Space's owner, who can remove or ban them.
  Reports go to the owner only, never to Aflos.
- If a Space's owner is the problem, leave the Space and block them.

## What others can still see

Friends you allow direct connections with can learn your IP address; turn
on **Hide my IP** in Privacy to send everything through a relay instead. A
relay's operator sees addresses and timing, never content. Anything you
post in a Space, its members can keep.

## You are responsible for what you share

Use Aflos lawfully, and only share what you have the right to share.
